# LawPDF 0.2.90 — Easier markup and reading

Draft release notes; publication and platform availability are still pending.

## What's new

- **Highlight an existing selection.** Select text in the original PDF, then choose a color or the underline swatch. The selected text is marked immediately; the tool stays ready for the next selection.
- **See the active tool at the cursor.** A small marker shows the selected color and whether it highlights or underlines. Text boxes and signatures have their own indicators, while the normal cursor keeps its precise selection point.
- **A slower Review reveal.** The first switch to Review takes about 3.2 seconds as words move into reading order. Click, scroll, or press a key to skip it; Reduce motion turns it off.
- **More flexible selection and navigation.** Drag across pages, double-click a word, triple-click a paragraph, or extend a selection with Shift+click. Keyboard reading shortcuts, PDF links and bookmarks, and supported footnote previews make longer documents easier to navigate.
- **Resume your work.** Recent documents can restore position, zoom, and view. Tabs can be reordered, closed with middle-click, or managed from their context menu.
- **Work with quotations and notes.** Copy selected text with a reviewable citation draft. The Notes panel lists LawPDF annotations and supported read-only marks from other PDF apps, and can export a Markdown digest.
- **Appearance and reading controls.** Choose light, dark, or system appearance; optionally dim original pages without changing the PDF or print output. System-voice reading offers speed and follow-along controls.
- **Performance and reliability.** Review layout draws visible content, page textures have a shared memory budget, and large eligible PDFs can show a temporary first-page preview while their editable snapshot is prepared. Failed opening and annotation saves have retry actions.
- The previously prepared Windows print controls and asynchronous Find interface are also included.

## Beta limits

Review reconstruction, detected citation metadata, paragraph boundaries, footnote references, and annotation extraction can be incomplete. Check important quotations and citations against the original. Imported third-party marks are read-only, and unsupported annotation types may be omitted.
