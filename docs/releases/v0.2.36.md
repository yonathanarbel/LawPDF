## LawPDF 0.2.36 — public beta for Windows and Mac

**Created by Professor Yonathan Arbel.** A free, open-source PDF reader for legal
scholarship, close reading, footnotes, and annotation.

### What's new

- A new reading interface places the document title, authors, and citation in a
  clear masthead. The annotation toolbar can move over the page.
- Review Mode places footnotes in the reading margin and hides the contents
  sidebar by default. Original view keeps PDF navigation available.
- A small coffee cup in the top bar opens a friendly support panel. Its button
  opens [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/lawpdf) in your browser.
  Support is entirely optional and unlocks no features. No document data is
  included in the link.
- The Mac Finder opening and save-aware closing fixes from 0.2.33 are retained.

LawPDF remains free under the MIT License, including commercial use.

Close the older app before installing. On Mac, extract the ZIP and replace
LawPDF.app in Applications. On Windows, run the installer or extract the entire
portable ZIP. Keep backups of important PDFs while using this beta.

The direct Windows downloads are not publisher-signed. The Mac app is ad hoc
signed and not notarized; first launch may require
[Apple's per-app approval process](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).
Do not disable operating-system security globally.

The [Microsoft Store edition](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9MZXSP0C423F)
receives Microsoft's signature and Store-managed updates. Version 0.2.36 was
submitted to Microsoft on September 27, 2026. Partner Center confirmed that the
update is in certification; this GitHub publication does not mean Microsoft has
finished certifying the update. Check the version offered by the Store.

### Beta status and privacy

Full VoiceOver/NVDA workflows, broad reading fidelity, large-document performance,
cloud-sync conflicts, field reliability, and interrupted automatic updates remain
under evaluation. Use the manual downloads above. Verify reconstructed quotations
and citations against the original PDF. The native PDF worker contains crashes
but is not an operating-system security sandbox.

Read the [privacy guide](https://github.com/yonathanarbel/LawPDF/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md)
before enabling network-backed AI features. Optional support is processed by Buy
Me a Coffee and its payment providers in your browser; LawPDF does not collect
payment details. [Report problems](https://github.com/yonathanarbel/LawPDF/issues)
without uploading confidential documents or private research.
